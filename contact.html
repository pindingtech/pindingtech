FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about contacting and working with us.

What is your typical response time?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please use our VIP hotline or WhatsApp for immediate assistance.

Do you offer phone support?

Yes, we offer phone support for VIP customers and urgent inquiries. Schedule a call by emailing us or request a callback through our mobile app.

What are your business hours?

Our office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM (China Standard Time). However, our mobile app support and online ticket system are available 24/7.

Can I schedule a video meeting?

Absolutely! We offer video meetings for product presentations, design consultations, and business discussions. Contact us via email with your preferred date and time.

Do you support international clients?

Yes, we serve clients globally across Europe, North America, Asia, and Australia. We offer multilingual support and international shipping to over 50 countries.

How can I track my order?

You can track your order through our mobile app or by contacting our support team. We provide real-time updates via email and SMS notifications.
