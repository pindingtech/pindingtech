Our Values

Core Principles That Drive Us

At Pinding Tech, our values are the foundation of everything we do. They guide our decisions, shape our culture, and define who we are as a company.

Innovation

We continuously push boundaries in furniture technology, embracing new ideas and cutting-edge solutions to create products that enhance modern living.

Quality

We never compromise on quality. Every piece of furniture and every service we provide meets the highest standards of craftsmanship and durability.

Customer First

Our customers are at the heart of everything we do. We listen, understand, and deliver solutions that exceed expectations.

Sustainability

We are committed to environmental responsibility, using sustainable materials and eco-friendly processes in our manufacturing operations.

Integrity

We conduct business with honesty and transparency, building lasting relationships based on trust and mutual respect.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in every detail, continuously improving our skills, processes, and products to achieve the highest standards.

Our History

Milestones of Success

From our founding to today, we have continuously evolved and grown, achieving significant milestones in furniture technology and service excellence.

2014

Company Founded

Pinding Tech was established in Changzhou with a vision to revolutionize furniture technology and bring innovative solutions to the global market.

2016

Manufacturing Expansion

We expanded our manufacturing facilities to include state-of-the-art production lines, increasing our capacity and improving quality control.

2018

R&D Center Established

Launched our dedicated research and development center, focusing on sustainable materials and smart furniture technology.

2020

International Expansion

Began serving international markets, establishing partnerships and distribution networks across multiple continents.

2022

Mobile App Launch

Released our innovative mobile application, bringing all services to customers' fingertips with seamless digital experience.

2024

Global Leadership

Recognized as a leader in furniture technology innovation, serving customers worldwide with comprehensive solutions and mobile platforms.
