FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our MOQ varies by product type. For custom furniture, we typically require a minimum of 10 units per design. Please contact our sales team for specific requirements.

How long does delivery take?

Standard orders are delivered within 4-6 weeks. Custom orders may take 6-10 weeks depending on complexity. Express options are available for urgent requirements.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by location. Contact us for a detailed shipping quote for your area.

What warranty do you provide?

We offer a comprehensive 5-year warranty on all furniture products covering manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse or normal wear and tear.

Can I customize furniture designs?

Absolutely! We specialize in custom furniture solutions. Share your requirements with our design team, and we'll create personalized designs that match your specifications.

Is the mobile app free to use?

Yes, our mobile application is free to download and use. Some premium features may require a subscription. The app is available on both Google Play and App Store.
